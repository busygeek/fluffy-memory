Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

Leonida Uploader is a personal tool used only by its owner to upload the owner's own videos to the owner's own accounts.

Data we access: basic profile info (display name, username) and permission to upload and publish videos to the authorized account.

How it is used: only to upload the owner's videos and confirm the posts. Access tokens are stored only on the owner's computer.

Sharing: no data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. No data from any other person is collected.

Deletion: access can be revoked at any time in TikTok under Settings and privacy > Security > Apps and services; the owner then deletes the stored tokens.

Contact: use the contact options on this site's owner's TikTok profile.
